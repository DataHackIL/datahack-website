Our programs are divided into 30% of mixed programs and roughly 70% programs for women only.

The program began as a part of a collaboration between the "Baot" community, aimed at increasing the presence of women in the high-tech industry and to create a work environment in which they can share knowledge and practice in groups, and DataHack.

The DataNights program is an educational program designed for members of the Data Science community In Israel.Today there are also programs for men and women and the goal has expanded to share knowledge in the Israeli data science community in general, in addition to the original goal of promoting women in the industry.

about us

This program is run by:

an association promoting data science and machine learning in Israel

Each cohort of the program focuses on a specific topic in data science and is made up of about 4 to 8 teaching sessions. Each one of these sessions includes a theoretical lecture and practical work in groups.

Achievements & Milestones

Speakers who gave their first talk with us have since been invited to speak at additional meetups and conferences.

Presenting as a speaker takes place in a
supportive and intimate environment, with constructive feedback provided throughout the talk preparation process.

DataNights alumni often mention their participation in job interviews and on their resumes.

Participants return to us as speakers in future cohorts.

Alumni repeatedly enroll in additional cohorts.
