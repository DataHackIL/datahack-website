OUR MISSION

Datahack is an Israeli non-profit dedicated to hacking diversity in data science and AI in Israel, and harnessing the field for social good.

Our Vision

We focus on data science literacy and education, empowerment and representation of underrepresented populations in Israel in AI, and on harnessing AI for social good.

Our Programs

DataCoach

A two-year coaching program, guiding Arab STEM students and graduates through advanced degrees and training to their first data science position.

DataNights

Empowering women in the AI industry, DataNights offer deep dive, hands-on courses around a specific topic over several weeks.

DataProject

Hands-on data science team projects connecting Arab students with industry partners for three months of real-world experience and portfolio-building.

DataTalks

A meetup series of deep technical ML talks pairing industry with academia speakers, each hosted at a different company.

DataConnectIL

A professional AI, Cyber Fraud & Cyber Security community for new and old Olim, aiming to overcome borders and time zones through shared language.

DataForBetter

Connecting social initiatives with experienced data scientists and diverse junior to design AI solutions  that do good.
