DataCoach Technion is a unique program built to help members of the Arab community in Israel enter the data science profession.

About the
program

The program will accompany you personally on your journey to the field of data science, while developing thinking skills, creating an extensive networking network, exposure to positions and areas of interest in the industry.

Together we will build the most correct personal path for you with the aim of becoming an outstanding data scientist.

You will attend weekly lectures & workshops on the Technion campus, covering both technical topics and soft skills. You will also get a personal mentor to work with you on your career path. Finally, you will work on a guided industry project to gain some practical experience in actual data science work!
