Program
Curriculum

A detailed program curriculum will be published soon. The program is built on three education axes:

Practical Training

The practical training pillar of the program will allow participants to practice the tools and principals they learned, on both DataCoach and their academic degree or other educational program, and build  a project solving real-world business program to showcase in interviews.

Soft Skills

The soft skills pillar of the program will bolster participants' capabilities crucial for finding employment, including interviewing skills, cv writing and project presentation principals, and will include interview dry-runs, cv building workshops and help from our mentors.

Applicative Knowledge & Skills

Frontal sessions and workshops will focus on introducing participants to practical industry tools and practices, and on expanding theoretical foundations in sub-domains which are especially relevant to data science work in the industry today.
